# Design Art Direction

Start from audience, product promise, emotional tone, and the dominant user action. Choose one coherent visual idea and express it through hierarchy, spacing, type, color, imagery, and motion.

Inspect existing brand and UI references before proposing changes. When useful, maintain a small `.design/` reference pack with source attribution and concrete cues.

Critique the rendered result, not just component structure. Identify the highest-impact visual mismatch and propose a specific correction. Respect accessibility and existing product constraints without collapsing into generic design.
